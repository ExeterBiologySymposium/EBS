Research that fits your school

Students can investigate biology through computational or literature-based projects. A standard laptop and an internet connection are enough to get started.

Our research guides and lectures can be completed asynchronously, so each student or classroom can set their own pace.

What participation involves

  • Share the opportunity with interested students.
  • Students choose a research question and work on a project with support from the EBS team and resource library.
  • Students prepare their work for presentation in person at Phillips Exeter Academy or online.

Practical details

Cost
Participation is free. Participants cover their own travel costs.
Time
Most students spend 1–2 hours per week, at their own pace.
Supervision
A teacher supervisor is not required. EBS guides students through the process.
Timing
April 24th, 2027.

Questions from schools

Can students work in teams?

Yes. Students may submit collaborative projects of up to three students. Every team member must contribute meaningfully and register individually.

Can students present their research elsewhere?

Yes. EBS does not restrict students from submitting their work to other science fairs or competitions.

Learning outcomes

Work through the curriculum using Python or RStudio. You will learn to analyze real data, document your decisions, and make figures for a research poster.

  • Read, clean, and explore biological datasets.
  • Use statistics to investigate a research question.
  • Explain findings, uncertainty, and limitations.
  • Package your analysis so another student can follow it.

Research questions and data types

Begin with a biological question. Identify what you will measure, which groups you will compare, and what evidence would help answer it.

Before you analyze

Identify your unit of observation and distinguish numerical measurements from categories. Record how the data were collected and what each column represents.

Review your research question

Can your available data address the question? State the variables, the comparison, and one limitation before running the analysis.

Spreadsheets and reproducibility

Keep an untouched copy of your original data. Document cleaning steps in a script so that your work can be checked and repeated.
