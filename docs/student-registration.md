# Student registration

Students sign in to EBS and submit the embedded form on [Student registration](https://www.exeterbiosymposium.page/student-signup.html). Google Forms fills the available EBS profile name and verified primary email. Students check the values before submitting or open the form in a new tab and enter them manually. The Team reviews applications in the form's private responses. Staff approve access in **EBS administration → Participants** and send welcome emails from the EBS inbox.

## Review and welcome a student

1. Open the student's form response and review the application. Use the **Researcher Email** to find their verified EBS account in **Participants → Add or invite participant**. Ask the student to confirm their EBS account email if you cannot match the application to a verified account.
2. Select **Accepted** and add the verified account, or use **Accept** on the student's Pending entry. Confirm that the account appears in **Accepted**. The website tries to provision the research folder and starter document after approval. Follow the status message if the student needs to link Google or folder setup needs a retry. An outstanding invitation alone does not confirm access. If approval reports **Accepted — cleanup needed**, retry the Pending cleanup as described in [organization administration](organization-admin.md#failure-recovery).
3. Check the EBS inbox's Sent folder and the Team's existing welcome records for that student before sending. Send the message below from `exeterbiosymposium@gmail.com` to the verified address. Replace `[Name]` with the student's name.
4. Record the date in the Team's private registration records. If delivery is uncertain, check Sent before trying again. Add the student to the private Researcher tracker if they are absent.

Keep student responses and staff tracking records private.
