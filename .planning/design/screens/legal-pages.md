# Terms and privacy pages

Use the established light EBS design with the current crimson tokens, Helvetica typography, shared header, and gray footer.

Each route has a page heading containing an eyebrow, one h1 (“Terms of Use” or “Privacy Notice”), a short explanatory lead, and a last-updated date. Beneath it, reuse `shell article-layout`: a labeled contents navigation alongside a readable article. Section anchors provide direct links to topics. Reuse current responsive behavior so the contents move above the article on smaller screens; keep the article readable without horizontal scrolling.

Use semantic section headings and ordinary text links with existing focus and underline treatment. Number section headings consistently with the approved copy. Keep paragraphs at the existing article font sizes (17px desktop, 16px mobile) and constrain reading width using existing `.reading` where appropriate. No hero images, cards, icons, consent modal, or new design system are needed.

Footer bottom: existing copyright and organizer text, then “Terms of Use,” “Privacy Notice,” and “Back to top.” Permit wrapping on intermediate widths and keep the existing stacked mobile layout. Both policy pages remain reachable without sign-in and link to each other, Contact, and the Academy privacy notice where relevant.
