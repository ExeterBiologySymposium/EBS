# EBS public and member layouts

Use white for primary reading surfaces, soft gray for supporting sections, and Exeter crimson for the strongest actions and selections. Use the existing scale, typeface, photographs and logos.

The homepage opens with a text-and-photo hero. The primary registration link sits below the visible event facts. Research guidance and the four research steps read in the normal page flow. The final image and registration link close the page.

The public header groups About, Participate and Help around the Schedule link. Search, registration and account controls use familiar labels. The mobile menu uses the same sections.

Register shows three audience rows. Student registration and Pending Account share an ordered application sequence. Accepted Account places the research folder before tools, events and secondary account actions.
