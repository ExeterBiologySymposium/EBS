# EBS website and member portal UX

Approved for local implementation. Student registration is the homepage priority. Schools and judges get distinct routes. Pending students can track their own external submission on this device. Accepted participants see their research folder first.

## Acceptance

- Event essentials are visible together. Navigation uses familiar labels, groups routes by visitor task, and shows protected destinations only to Accepted participants.
- Registration identifies the three audiences, explains the same-email Google Forms handoff, and distinguishes a self-reported submission from server-verified approval.
- The reversible checkbox is scoped to the 2027 event and the signed-in account. The server receives no checkbox data.
- Member lessons have contents links where appropriate. Dataset filters sit beside results on desktop and above results on mobile.
- Existing publication, preview, routing, form and access contracts continue to work.
