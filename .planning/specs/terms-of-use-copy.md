## 2. Our purpose

EBS helps middle and high school students take their first steps in biological research. Through free learning resources, structured guidance, and a supportive symposium, students develop research questions, analyze evidence, and present original dry-lab biological research to peers and judges.

Dry-lab research uses computers, public datasets, statistical methods, computational tools, and published literature rather than physical laboratory experiments. Our aim is to make rigorous research more accessible to students who may not have laboratory access or prior research experience, while encouraging curiosity, academic integrity, and clear scientific communication.

EBS participation is free. Participants remain responsible for their own equipment, internet access, travel, and other personal expenses unless we expressly state otherwise. Participation does not guarantee a presentation place, award, publication, academic credit, or admission to Phillips Exeter Academy or another institution.

## 7. Research integrity and appropriate projects

Research submitted to EBS must follow the published project requirements. Describe your own contributions accurately, acknowledge collaborators and assistance, cite sources, and comply with dataset and software licenses. Do not plagiarize, fabricate or falsify data, or present another person's work as your own.

Keep records sufficient to explain your methods and support your results. Explain limitations and do not present exploratory findings as established conclusions. Disclose assistance from software, automated tools, or other people where required by the submission instructions or needed to make your contribution clear.

EBS supports dry-lab research. Participation does not provide approval to conduct experiments involving people, animals, biological specimens, or hazardous materials, or to use restricted data. Obtain any required ethical, institutional, data-access, or other permissions independently before beginning work that requires them. Do not upload identifiable patient records, private genetic information, passwords, or other sensitive information to EBS tools.
